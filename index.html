Our Philosophy

Why 2 Hours on Sunday Changes Everything

We believe you don't need years of specialized training to make an indelible mark on a child's life. What they need most is genuine human presence, patience, and warmth.

PILLAR 01

The Reading Gap

Millions of first-generation learners in Jammu attend school but struggle to read basic storybooks. Without 1-on-1 reading practice, school becomes daunting rather than exciting.

PILLAR 02

The Sunday Bridge

We bring energetic college students and working professionals right into neighborhood centers. Each mentor sits with 2–3 kids, turning learning into a joyful Sunday ritual.

PILLAR 03

The Ripple of Self-Belief

When a child masters their first paragraph or solves a math puzzle, their posture changes. They see school as an adventure and believe they can dream big.

Ways You Can Contribute

Three Hands-on Volunteer Roles

Pick what excites you most. We handle the community permissions, venue logistics, and learning materials.

Teaching Track

Foundational Literacy & Math

Teach phonics, story comprehension, basic Hindi/English reading, and joyful mental arithmetic games to kids aged 6 to 14.

Creative Track

Arts, Science & Joyful Play

Lead hands-on origami workshops, simple science demonstrations, theater games, and storytelling circles that spark wild curiosity.

Resource Track

Book & Stationery Drives

Help collect and curate storybooks, distribute fresh notebooks and pencils, and set up mini reading corners in community classrooms.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior teaching experience to volunteer? +
Not at all! We provide structured, fun activity sheets and flashcards. What the children need most is patience, warmth, and someone who listens to them read.
Can I volunteer only on alternate weekends? +
Yes! We understand you have college or work commitments. You can sign up for whichever Sunday fits your schedule.
Can I donate storybooks or stationery directly? +
Absolutely! We happily welcome storybooks, drawing books, crayons, and notebooks. Reach out on our WhatsApp or bring them to any Sunday drive.
Is there any registration fee? +
Zero fees. Rise with Ren Setu is 100% free and open for all community members and children.
